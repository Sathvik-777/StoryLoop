Every evening, Mr. Ellis sat alone, the silence pressing heavy around him. One cold night, a scruffy dog appeared at his doorstep, timid eyes searching for warmth. Hesitant but hopeful, he opened the door. Days passed—quiet companionship blossomed, filling empty rooms with soft paws and gentle nudges. In that shared stillness, loneliness softened, replaced by a bond that wordlessly said: you’re not alone anymore. As the dog rested its head on his lap, Mr. Ellis finally smiled—because some friendships heal what time cannot.
