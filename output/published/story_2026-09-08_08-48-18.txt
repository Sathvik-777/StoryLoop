They say every apartment has its secrets. But mine? It had one I wasn’t prepared for.  

It started with a strange draft—cold air where no window should be. Puzzled, I pressed my hand against the far wall in the living room. A faint click echoed.  

The wall shifted, revealing a dark, narrow passage. Heart racing, I stepped inside. The hidden room was empty… except for one thing: an old photo album.  

Flipping through, I found pictures of me—taken years ago, in this very apartment. But I’d never lived here before.  

Suddenly, the door slammed shut behind me. And the first page showed a message: *“Welcome back.”*  

Was this a trap? Or a home waiting to be found?
