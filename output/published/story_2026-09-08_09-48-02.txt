At dawn, a figure races through the mist, clutching a fragile heartbeat. Each day, they care for a silver-winged stranger, feeding hope with whispered lullabies and steady hands. But shadows creep in—the bird’s light flickers, growing faint with every struggle. Yet in that fading glow, a quiet bond deepens—two souls caught in a dance of courage and farewell. Flight may never come, but their love soars above pain, a lasting proof that even in loss, hope takes flight.
