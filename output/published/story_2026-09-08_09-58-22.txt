In a quiet hospital room, an elderly man sits alone, his eyes distant and sad. A young nurse enters, her smile warm but gentle. She notices his loneliness and quietly places a small photo album beside him. Day by day, she visits with stories, laughter, and soft songs. Slowly, his eyes brighten, memories stirring hope and joy. On his final day, she holds his hand, tears mingling with a smile—knowing her kindness gave him peace and a spark of happiness when he needed it most. Sometimes, the greatest healing is simply being there.
