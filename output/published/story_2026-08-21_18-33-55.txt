The first night in his new apartment, Mark heard a faint tapping behind the living room wall. At first, he thought it was just the pipes—until the tapping turned into a rhythmic pattern, almost like a code. Driven by a strange pull, he pressed along the wall, searching for a weak spot. Suddenly, a section gave way, revealing a narrow, dusty hallway he never knew existed. Heart racing, he stepped inside and found a room filled with old photographs and letters—belonging to the previous tenant. One letter stood out: it was addressed to Mark. It read, "If you’re reading this, the walls are listening. Trust no one. The secret must stay hidden." That’s when the tapping started again—this time, from inside the hidden room.
