A tiny, shivering puppy is left alone on a cold, empty street. His eyes search desperately for a home that never comes. Days pass—empty bowls, lonely nights. Just when hope seems lost, a gentle hand reaches down, offering warmth and kindness. The puppy's timid tail begins to wag. Though the past aches, this small act of love kindles a new beginning—proof that even the smallest hearts can heal with a little compassion.
